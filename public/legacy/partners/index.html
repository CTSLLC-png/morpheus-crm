Program terms

The essentials before you apply

  • Qualified saleA completed Legacy Path lifetime-access purchase attributed to your assigned tracked link that is not refunded, disputed, charged back, fraudulent or a self-referral.
  • Commission and paymentThe standard commission is $5 per qualified sale. Approved commissions are calculated after the 30-day refund period and paid monthly using the payment method CTS makes available to the partner.
  • AttributionCTS records referral information through assigned tracked links. The CTS transaction record controls commission eligibility when more than one source claims a sale.
  • DisclosurePartners must clearly disclose the paid relationship in every promotional post or video and comply with each platform's branded-content rules.
  • Truthful promotionPartners may share honest opinions and approved product facts. False claims, invented testimonials, spam, misleading financial promises and impersonation are prohibited.
  • Brand useLegacy Path and CTS names, marks and supplied materials may be used only to promote the program during an active partnership. Materials may not be altered in a way that changes their meaning.
  • Independent relationshipPartners are independent contractors responsible for their own taxes, records and legal obligations. Participation does not create employment, agency, franchise or investment-adviser status.
  • Program changesCTS may review traffic quality, withhold ineligible commissions, end a partnership or update program terms. Material changes apply prospectively after notice to active partners.

The full written partner agreement provided at approval controls participation. Applying does not guarantee acceptance.
